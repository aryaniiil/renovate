## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the discretion, architectural rigor, and intimate tactility of a private residential renovation house. Conceived for ultra-high-net-worth patrons and private commissions, the visual atmosphere departs from digital conventions, drawing instead from private monograph publishing, curated gallery catalogs, and restored historic residences.

Key aesthetic pillars:
- **Quiet Restraint:** Generous breathing room, minimal interactive clutter, and a serene pace of discovery.
- **Architectural Craftsmanship:** Structural balance underpinned by hairline metalwork, warm limestone planes, and subtle physical depth.
- **Intimacy & Discretion:** Subtle, deliberate transitions that evoke entering a secluded salon or private library.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from tactile architectural finishes: dressed limestone, hand-rubbed patinated bronze, and deep oxblood lacquer.

- **Background Canvas (`#EAE4DA`):** Raw, warm limestone establishing a quiet, non-digital foundation.
- **Surfaces & Cards (`#F9F6F0`):** Fine warm plaster and handmade parchment, layered over the limestone canvas to elevate content softly.
- **Primary / Statement (`#4A1B21`):** Deep heritage oxblood reserved for singular moments of emphasis, exclusive invitations, active key actions, and curated immersive panels.
- **Bronze (`#A27B4F`):** Architectural joinery tone used for delicate hairlines, structural indices, metadata, and understated numerals.
- **Text & Foundations (`#231B18`):** Deep roasted espresso anchoring headlines and narrative body text without the harshness of pure black.
- **Secondary Neutral (`#7C7068`):** Muted stone pigment for secondary prose, metadata, and supporting labels.
- **Borders & Dividers:** Bronze rendered at `30%` to `35%` alpha (`rgba(162, 123, 79, 0.32)`), simulating hand-scribed metal joints.

## Typography

The typographical cadence balances classical editorial warmth with contemporary architectural clarity.

- **Editorial Headlines (`EB Garamond`):** Used in regular weights with selective italicization for poetic, tactile words (e.g., *residence*, *craft*, *light*). Never forced into bold weights.
- **System Body (`Hanken Grotesk`):** Calibrated to exactly `17px` (`1.0625rem`) with an open `28px` (`1.75rem`) line height for comfortable, publication-grade reading.
- **Eyebrows & Architectural Indices:** Set strictly in `12px` uppercase `Hanken Grotesk`, tracked widely at `0.16em` and rendered in bronze (`#A27B4F`).
- **Numerals & Ordinals:** Editorial numbers within lists, chronological dates, and project indices employ lining numerals in bronze or warm espresso.

## Layout & Spacing

The structural layout mirrors classical pavilion proportions, employing an expansive 12-column grid anchored by generous negative space.

- **Rhythm & Vertical Flow:** Major structural sections maintain an expansive vertical distance of `140px` (`8.75rem`), establishing a serene, unhurried reading cadence. On mobile devices, this eases gracefully to `80px` (`5rem`).
- **Grid Structure:** A 12-column desktop grid with a `1440px` maximum bounding box. Layouts frequently occupy asymmetric columns (e.g., 5-column editorial text flanking a 7-column architectural view).
- **Hairlines:** Thin architectural rule lines (`1px` height, `#A27B4F` at 32% opacity) structure section breaks, metadata shelves, and footers without creating visual heaviness.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on quiet tonal layering and warm, diffuse illumination rather than cold mechanical elevations.

- **Atmospheric Layering:** The primary canvas (`#EAE4DA`) acts as the natural floor. Interactive folios, client cards, and dialogue overlays lift onto `#F9F6F0`.
- **Warm-Tinted Floating Shadows:** Shadows emulate low afternoon light through linen curtains. They are deeply diffused, extended in radius, and tinted with warm espresso:
  - *Resting Card:* `0 12px 36px -8px rgba(35, 27, 24, 0.05), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(35, 27, 24, 0.03)`
  - *Elevated / Focus Overlay:* `0 24px 60px -12px rgba(35, 27, 24, 0.08), 0 8px 20px -4px rgba(35, 27, 24, 0.04)`
- **Statement Depth:** In the inverted oxblood section (`#4A1B21`), surfaces recede into rich tonal darkness with faint bronze-lit borders (`rgba(162, 123, 79, 0.25)`) and zero drop shadows.

## Shapes

The geometric vernacular uses precise, tailored curvature reflecting hand-shaped stone, carved timber, and sculpted plaster.

- **Large Project Portals & Primary Imagery:** Sculpted at `36px` corner radius to evoke framed museum viewfinders.
- **Folio Cards & Containers:** Structured at `28px` corner radius.
- **Form Controls & Inputs:** Softened at `16px` corner radius.
- **Buttons, Badges & Interactive Tags:** Formed into full pill shapes (`9999px`), imparting a soft, tactile, pebble-like presence under hand.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary Invitation Button:** Pill-shaped silhouette in deep oxblood (`#4A1B21`) with warm white text (`#F9F6F0`). Padded with `14px 32px`. Hover shifts tonally to a slightly deeper oxblood with a subtle warm glow (`rgba(74, 27, 33, 0.15)`).
- **Secondary / Atelier Inquiries:** Pill-shaped transparent container bounded by a `1px` bronze hairline (`rgba(162, 123, 79, 0.4)`), featuring deep espresso text and an understated hover tint of `rgba(162, 123, 79, 0.08)`.
- **Text Anchors:** Underlined with a delicate bronze stroke offset by `6px`, transitioning opacity on hover.

### Cards & Folio Enclosures
- Grounded on warm white surfaces (`#F9F6F0`) with `28px` rounded corners and the signature warm floating shadow.
- Interior padding ranges from `32px` to `48px`. Bound optionally by a hairline border (`rgba(162, 123, 79, 0.2)`) on subtle canvas transitions.

### Form Inputs & Client Discretion Fields
- Enclosed with `16px` curvature, resting on pure warm white surfaces.
- Subtle `1px` border in `#EAE4DA` shifting to focused bronze (`#A27B4F`) without harsh glow rings.
- Placeholder copy set in muted `#7C7068`, transitioning to `#231B18` when populated.

### Chips & Discretion Badges
- Pill-shaped (`9999px`) badges using limestone backgrounds (`#EAE4DA`) or faint bronze washes (`rgba(162, 123, 79, 0.12)`).
- Typography strictly uppercase `12px` tracked at `0.16em`.

### Selection Controls
- **Radio Marks & Checkboxes:** Slender `16px` discs and soft squares bounded in bronze (`#A27B4F`). Active indicators settle in oxblood (`#4A1B21`) with warm white central glyphs.

### Statement Section & Footer Architecture
- **Curated Oxblood Chamber:** A singular immersive section enveloped entirely in `#4A1B21`, setting cream headlines (`#F9F6F0`), bronze details (`#A27B4F`), and muted plaster body copy (`#EAE4DA`).
- **Atelier Footer:** Finished in deep roasted espresso (`#231B18`) with hairline dividers, discreet archival index marks, and muted stone typography.
