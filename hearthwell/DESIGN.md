## Brand & Style

This design system expresses quiet authority, architectural precision, and domestic warmth. Created for high-end residential renovation, the aesthetic blends the restraint of an architectural monograph with the sensory comfort of natural building materials (lime wash, oiled timber, burnished brass, and honed soapstone).

The emotional target is reassuring calm and unhurried discernment. The experience should feel measured, spacious, and tactile—avoiding both cold minimalist tech cliches and overly ornate traditional tropes. 

### Visual Expression
- **Aesthetic Movement:** Warm Editorial Minimalism with tactile, classical undertones.
- **Imagery:** Generously framed lifestyle and architectural documentation, utilizing natural directional light, rich natural textures, and precise geometry.
- **Iconography:** Monoline architectural symbols defined strictly with a 1.5px stroke, rounded terminals, and clear structural silhouettes.

## Colors

The palette is derived from natural, enduring construction finishes. Warm ivory replaces clinical whites, forest greens evoke landscaped garden connections, and brushed brass lends structural warmth to interactive accents.

### Palette Roles
- **Base Canvas (`#F6F2EA`):** Warm Ivory. Applied to page bodies to reduce glare and convey warmth.
- **Card / Primary Surface (`#FFFFFF`):** Crisp pure lime plaster white, providing focal elevation above the warm ivory canvas.
- **Secondary Surface (`#ECE5D8`):** Sand. Used for secondary containers, stat boxes, alternating content sections, and chip tags.
- **Primary Brand (`#23412F`):** Deep Forest Green. Used for key headlines, dominant interactive triggers, and grounding structural blocks.
- **Accent (`#B8935A`):** Brushed Brass / Antique Gold. Reserved for subtle emphasis, active states, progress indicators, rating ornaments, and metadata badges.
- **Text Primary (`#1B1F1D`):** Rich charcoal-black with slight warm green undertones. Ensures legible contrast without the harshness of pure `#000000`.
- **Text Secondary / Muted (`#5E655F`):** Weathered stone grey for supportive captions, subheadings, and secondary labels.
- **Borders (`#E4DDD0`):** Subtle limestone outlines at a consistent 1px stroke.
- **Inverted Canvas (`#1B1F1D`):** Deep charcoal ground reserved specifically for full-bleed footers and select dramatic hero takeovers.

## Typography

The type system creates deliberate contrast between the literary refinement of the editorial serif and the neutral, functional precision of the sans-serif.

- **Headlines:** Set in `Newsreader` (standing in for editorial serif display type) at weight 500. Letter tracking is kept consistently snug (-0.015em to -0.025em) to maintain typographic cohesion and architectural gravitas.
- **Body & Interface:** Set in `Inter`. Chosen for supreme optical clarity, open counters, and high legibility across long-form renovation proposals, scope documents, and interactive forms.
- **Hierarchy Rules:** Display and headline levels should never be set in all caps. Reserve uppercase treatments exclusively for `label-sm` and `caption` (such as category kickers, tags, or phase markers) with wide tracking (+0.04em to +0.06em).

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts adhere to an architectural grid prioritizing generous breathing room and asymmetrical balance. 

### Grid & Canvas Structure
- **Desktop (≥ 1200px):** 12-column layout, max-width `1360px`, `gutter-lg` (40px), and `margin-lg` (64px).
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8-column layout, fluid margins (`margin-md`: 40px), `gutter` (24px).
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid layout, `margin` (20px), `gutter` (16px).

### Spacing Principles
- **Editorial Pacing:** Avoid density. Modules should be separated by substantial vertical gaps (`space-xl` to double `space-xl`) to establish an unhurried cadence.
- **Micro Spacing:** Form controls, interactive rows, and cards utilize consistent internal padding based on `space-md` (20px) to `space-lg` (32px), creating a sense of luxury through deliberate negative space.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established through soft, multi-layered natural light and tactile surface transitions rather than aggressive artificial drops.

### Shadow Characteristics
- **Tone & Ambient Quality:** Shadows are never neutral grey. They carry a faint warm green-charcoal undertone (`rgba(27, 31, 29, 0.04)` to `rgba(27, 31, 29, 0.08)`).
- **Subtle Surface Lift (Cards & Modules):**
  - Layer 1: `0 2px 4px rgba(27, 31, 29, 0.02)`
  - Layer 2: `0 8px 24px rgba(27, 31, 29, 0.04)`
- **Hover & Active Overlay Lift (Modals, Hovered Cards):**
  - Layer 1: `0 4px 8px rgba(27, 31, 29, 0.03)`
  - Layer 2: `0 20px 48px rgba(27, 31, 29, 0.07)`
- **Architectural Boundary:** Shadows are paired with a 1px perimeter border in `#E4DDD0` to anchor edges crisp and clean against the `#F6F2EA` canvas.

## Shapes

The visual geometry balances soft, inviting rounded corners with structural precision:

- **Cards & Primary Modules:** Fixed corner radius of `24px` (`rounded-xl`).
- **Feature Image Containers & Media:** Fixed corner radius of `32px` to evoke architectural portals and custom masonry frames.
- **Input Fields & Dropdowns:** Fixed corner radius of `14px` for ergonomic hand-feel.
- **Buttons & Small Badges:** Fully pill-shaped (`9999px`) to create an immediate tactile affordance that stands out against architectural rectangular layouts.
- **Dividers & Lines:** Clean, unembellished 1px continuous hairline strokes.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), `#23412F` background, `#FFFFFF` text. Padding: `14px 28px`. Hover: shifts subtly to `#1B1F1D` with layered elevation lift.
- **Accent:** Pill-shaped, `#B8935A` background, `#FFFFFF` text. Used for primary conversion points (e.g., "Schedule Consultation").
- **Secondary (Outline):** Pill-shaped, 1px border in `#23412F`, text in `#23412F`, transparent background. On hover: fills with `#ECE5D8`.
- **Text Link:** Underlined in `#B8935A` with a 1px offset, accompanied by a 1.5px stroke directional arrow icon.

### Form Inputs
- Height: `52px`, corner radius: `14px`.
- Background: `#FFFFFF`, border: `1px solid #E4DDD0`, text: `#1B1F1D`.
- Padding: `0 18px`.
- Focus State: Border color transitions to `#23412F` with a soft `0 0 0 3px rgba(35, 65, 47, 0.1)` focus halo.
- Labels: Placed above input, rendered in `label-md` using `#1B1F1D`.

### Cards
- Corner radius: `24px`.
- Background: Pure `#FFFFFF` resting on `#F6F2EA` ground.
- Border: `1px solid #E4DDD0`.
- Padding: `32px` desktop, `24px` mobile.
- Interior rhythm: Image frame at top (radius `16px`), followed by metadata in `#B8935A`, headline in `headline-sm`, and description in `body-md`.

### Chips & Badges
- Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), height: `30px`, padding: `4px 14px`.
- Default: `#ECE5D8` background, `#1B1F1D` text, no border.
- Active/Selected: `#23412F` background, `#FFFFFF` text.
- Metadata Kicker: `#F6F2EA` background with a 1px `#B8935A` border, text in `#B8935A`, styled uppercase in `caption`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Size: `20px x 20px`.
- Radius: `6px` for checkboxes, circular (`rounded-full`) for radios.
- Unchecked: `1.5px solid #E4DDD0` on `#FFFFFF` ground.
- Checked: `#23412F` background with a `#FFFFFF` checkmark/dot (1.5px stroke weight).

### Specialty Components: Before & After Split Visualizer
- Container: Large image viewport with `32px` rounded radius and a 1px border in `#E4DDD0`.
- Handle: Vertical hairlike brass divider (`#B8935A`) with a circular pill dragger (`40px x 40px`, `#FFFFFF`, containing dual monoline chevrons).
