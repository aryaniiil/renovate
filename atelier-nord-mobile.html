The Protocol

The Atelier Method

Four stages executed with quiet discipline. No compromises, no hidden revisions.

01 Weeks 01–04

Discover & Audit

Historical analysis, thermal envelope diagnostics, and spatial micro-climate mapping before a single sketch is drawn.

02 Weeks 05–12

Compose & Procure

Architectural blueprinting, material sample workshops, and quarry block reservation directly with European artisans.

03 Months 04–10

Build & Refine

In-house joiners and specialist stonemasons on-site with full bi-weekly photographic documentation and laser tolerance tests.

04 Final Milestone

Hand Over & Monograph

Comprehensive spatial commissioning, bespoke leather-bound maintenance archive, and lifetime care advisory.
