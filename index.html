Clarity & Confidence

Frequently asked questions.

How does your fixed-price contract guarantee work?

expand_more
Before signing your renovation agreement, we conduct a millimeter-accurate 3D scan and pre-demolition wall cavity inspection. This allows us to account for plumbing routes, electrical loads, and structural requirements beforehand. Once the scope is finalized, our price is locked. If unexpected standard structural challenges arise, Hearth & Grain absorbs that cost—not you.

Can we continue living in our home during the renovation?

expand_more
Yes, for kitchen and single bathroom projects, the vast majority of our clients remain in their homes. We erect negative-pressure ZipWall dust barriers with commercial HEPA air scrubbers, lay durable neoprene ram board on all pathways, and adhere to strict working hours (8:30 AM to 4:30 PM). For whole-home transformations involving total subfloor plumbing, temporary relocation may be recommended.

Who manages municipal permits and engineering stamps?

expand_more
We manage 100% of the administrative process. Our in-house licensed architectural staff produces the stamped plan sets, submits directly to your local municipal building department, coordinates structural engineer reviews, and schedules all inspector visits throughout construction.

What is the typical timeline from contract to completion?

expand_more
Design and permitting generally spans 3 to 5 weeks. Once construction begins, a master bathroom takes 3 to 4 weeks, a gourmet architectural kitchen takes 5 to 7 weeks, and whole-home remodeling takes 10 to 14 weeks. Your timeline is scheduled to the exact day in our digital portal.

Can we supply our own vintage or custom materials?

expand_more
Absolutely. We regularly incorporate heirloom fixtures, reclaimed timber mantels, and specialized vintage stone vessels. Our architectural team reviews specifications in advance to ensure plumbing and structural compatibility.

What does the 10-year workmanship warranty cover?

expand_more
Our warranty covers all structural framing, waterproofing assemblies, cabinetry joinery integrity, tile mortar bonding, electrical rough-ins, and plumbing supply lines. If a drawer slide slips or a waterproof membrane shows defect, our team repairs it at zero expense to you.
