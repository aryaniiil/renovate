The Journey

A calm process, from first conversation to last detail.

We insulate clients from site disruption through rigorous archival drawings, dedicated master builders, and single-source project stewardship.

Begin the conversation arrow_forward
Mutual alignment begins with an unhurried consultation at your current home or our St. James’s studio. We examine your daily domestic rituals, timeline parameters, and architectural aspirations before formalising terms.
Comprehensive volumetric drawings, 1:1 scale joinery details, and hand-rendered spatial plans. We assemble physical material trays with limestone, patinated metals, and woven textiles for tactile approval.
Full tender documentation with zero ambiguity. Every fitting, mechanical line, acoustic membrane, and stone slab is pre-purchased and quarantined in our depot to avert project delays.
Execution handled exclusively by our permanent guild of master carpenters, stonemasons, and engineers. We provide weekly photographic dispatches and private site walkthroughs.
A bespoke physical leather binder of warranties, maintenance protocols for all unlacquered metals and untreated stones, and scheduled seasonal check-ins during the first two years of residency.
